In 2026, I carried out research for my Master's Thesis from January to November.

The final paper is published at this link:
https://scholarworks.lib.csusb.edu/etd/2553/

With permission, I extracted ten years of point-of-sale transactions from a small grocery store operated by an adjoining farm.  I appended the transactions and transformed them with Pandas.  I then used scikit-learn to develop and run machine learning regression models, including Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and LSTM.  I was able to predict the sale of 25 lb. bags of oranges with a moderate level of accuracy.  Limitations that I discovered included intermittent sales patterns and somewhat inconsistent product availability which weakened the models' abilities to achieve strong forecasting power.
